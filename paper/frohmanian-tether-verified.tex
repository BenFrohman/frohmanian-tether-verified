\begin{abstract}
We audit the claims of the technical compilation \emph{The Frohmanian Symplectic Tether: Architecture, Reduced Jacobi Identity, and the Frohmanian Curve} (Zenodo record 23942466) against a Lean~4/Mathlib formalization. Every claim is classified as \emph{proved}, \emph{conditional} (proved under explicitly stated hypotheses), \emph{refuted} (its negation is machine-checked), or \emph{not formalized}. The library builds without \texttt{sorry}, and the 37 key theorems depend only on the standard axioms \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, and \texttt{Quot.sound}. We report proved and conditional results only as such. The compilation's claims of Jacobi vanishing on the reduced orbit and of energy-budget damping are refuted. Its Clay-standard global regularity claim is \textbf{not} established: it holds only conditionally on open hypotheses. This paper makes no Millennium Prize claim.
\end{abstract}

\section{Proved results}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Locality and nonpositive self-pairing (C1/C3).} \lean{frohmanianTether_self_nonpos}, \lean{tetherKernel_self_nonpos}.
\item \textbf{Exact degeneracy on kinetic energy (C2).} \lean{frohmanianTether_degenerate}, \lean{tetherKernel_degenerates_on_kinetic_energy}.
\item \textbf{Nine-term Jacobi expansion.} \lean{jacobiator_nine_terms} and \lean{jacobi_iff_mixed_cancel}: the tethered jacobiator vanishes iff the mixed terms cancel. See also \lean{FieldJacobi.jacT_eq_mixedSum} and \lean{FieldJacobi.jacF_eq_zero}.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conditional results}
Each holds only under the hypotheses stated in Lean.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Negative feedback with a fixed coefficient (C3).} This is finite-dimensional and assumes the Route-A hypothesis: \lean{frohmanianTether_feedback}, \lean{feedback_fixes_coefficient}, \lean{routeA_determines_kappa}.
\item \textbf{Uniqueness (C4/C5).} This holds within the minimal admissible class only: \lean{uniqueness_of_minimal_metric_tether}.
\item \textbf{Chevalley--Eilenberg closure.} $d^2=0$ holds when $B$ is a coboundary (\lean{CE.d2_d1}, \lean{CE.cocycle_of_coboundary}). The symmetric tether is \emph{not} a CE cochain (\lean{CE.symPart_not_cochain}).
\item \textbf{Jacobi in special cases.} It holds for central $u$ (\lean{jacobiator_eq_zero_of_central}) and for the $x$-only shear class (\lean{FieldJacobi.shearY_jacobi_holds}). \lean{tethered_jacobi_identity} assumes the vanishing.
\item \textbf{Majorant and quartic absorption.} This holds given the ODE inequality: \lean{comparison_bound}, \lean{layer2_bound}, \lean{Riccati.tethered_ceiling}. Without absorption, Riccati blow-up occurs: \lean{Riccati.riccati_blowup}.
\item \textbf{Maximum-principle feedback.} These are pointwise lemmas: \lean{viscous_nonpos_at_max}, \lean{periodic_exists_max}.
\item \textbf{Global regularity.} \lean{frohmanian_symplectic_tether_theorem} and \lean{frohmanian_symplectic_tether_theorem_global} derive smoothness from hypotheses, including a local-continuation hypothesis, that are \emph{open}. They do not resolve the Clay problem.
\end{itemize}

\section{Refuted claims}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The tether damps the energy budget.} This is refuted. C2 forces $B(M,H)=0$, so the tether contributes nothing to $dH/dt$: \lean{ObstructionAudit.flow_contribution_zero_of_C2}, \lean{ObstructionAudit.bilin_kernel_of_nonpos_of_self_zero}, \lean{ObstructionAudit.energy_conservation_not_C2}.
\item \textbf{Jacobi vanishes on the reduced (divergence-free) orbit.} This is refuted. It fails for the divergence-free field $u=(\sin y,0,0)$: \lean{JacobiWitness.tethered_jacobiator_ne_zero}, \lean{FieldJacobi.shearY_jacobi_fails_transverse}, \lean{FieldJacobi.divFree_aZ}. The bracket is metriplectic, not Poisson (\lean{FieldMetriplectic.dissF_nonpos}).
\item \textbf{The shear example shows the reduced orbit is necessary.} This is partly refuted. The failure for the non-divergence-free shear is correct (\lean{FieldJacobi.shearX_jacobi_fails}, \lean{FieldJacobi.not_divFree_uX}), but divergence-free fields also fail.
\end{itemize}

\section{Open and not formalized}
The following have no Lean counterpart and remain open:
\begin{itemize}
\item Alain's Curve (\S2);
\item the Frohmanian Curve $J_{\mathrm{red}}=0$ (\S4), for which the compilation gives no explicit $J_{\mathrm{red}}$;
\item the symplectic, Melnikov, spectral, and holographic representations (\S5);
\item the explicit majorant ODE $y'=Cy^2\sqrt{y^3}+\tfrac{\pi}{4}C_{\mathrm{abs}}$.
\end{itemize}
The Clay-standard global regularity claim is open.

\section{Conclusion}
The C1--C3 architecture and the algebraic Jacobi structure are machine-checked. Its two central dynamical claims, reduced-orbit Jacobi and energy damping, are false as stated. Global regularity remains conditional on open hypotheses.
