\begin{abstract}
We audit the claims of the technical compilation \emph{The Frohmanian Symplectic Tether: Architecture, Reduced Jacobi Identity, and the Frohmanian Curve} (Zenodo record 23942466) against a Lean~4/Mathlib formalization. Every claim is classified as \emph{proved}, \emph{conditional} (proved under explicitly stated hypotheses), \emph{refuted} (its negation is machine-checked), or \emph{not formalized}. The library builds without \texttt{sorry}, and the 38 key theorems depend only on the standard axioms \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, and \texttt{Quot.sound}. We report proved and conditional results only as such. The compilation's claims of Jacobi vanishing on the reduced orbit and of energy-budget damping are refuted. Its Clay-standard global regularity claim is \textbf{not} established: it holds only conditionally on open hypotheses. This paper makes no Millennium Prize claim.
\end{abstract}

\section{Proved results}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Locality (C1) and nonpositive self-pairing (C3).} In the abstract model, every linear isometry $g$ of $H$ with $gu=u$ preserves the tether: \lean{frohmanianTether_invariant}, via \lean{piU_comm_isometry}. If $\kappa\ge0$ and $w\ge0$, then $\mathfrak{T}_F[\kappa,w,u](F,F)\le0$ (\lean{frohmanianTether_self_nonpos}); the same conclusion holds under the weaker hypothesis $\kappa w\ge0$ (\lean{frohmanianTether_self_nonpos_of_mul_nonneg}). Independently, the kernel pairing satisfies $\mathrm{TetherKernel}\,\omega\,F\,F\le0$ with no extra hypothesis (\lean{tetherKernel_self_nonpos}).
\item \textbf{Exact degeneracy on kinetic energy (C2).} In the abstract model, $\mathfrak{T}_F[\kappa,w,u](F,u)=0$ for every $F$ (\lean{frohmanianTether_degenerate}), unconditionally in $u$. In the kernel model, $\mathrm{TetherKernel}\,\omega\,F\,\mathrm{KineticEnergyHamiltonian}=0$ is proved only under three hypotheses: the functional derivative of the kinetic-energy Hamiltonian equals the reconstructed velocity, that velocity is divergence-free, and its squared speed is integrable (\lean{tetherKernel_degenerates_on_kinetic_energy}). Those three hypotheses remain assumptions of that theorem; the identity is not proved without them.
\item \textbf{Nine-term Jacobi expansion.} For a classical bracket $P$ (\lean{IsClassicalBracket}), the jacobiator of the tethered bracket expands as the sum of the classical jacobiator, the cyclic mixed terms, and the cyclic triple tether terms (\lean{jacobiator_nine_terms}). The tethered jacobiator vanishes on a set $R$ if and only if the mixed terms cancel on $R$ (\lean{jacobi_iff_mixed_cancel}). In the field model, the classical field jacobiator is identically zero (\lean{FieldJacobi.jacF_eq_zero}), and the tethered field jacobiator equals the mixed sum whenever $\mathrm{pr}\,u\,u\neq0$ (\lean{FieldJacobi.jacT_eq_mixedSum}). This is an expansion identity, not a proof that the jacobiator vanishes in general.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conditional results}
Each holds only under the hypotheses stated in Lean; those hypotheses are recorded here and remain open unless a proved theorem discharges them.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Negative feedback with a fixed coefficient (C3).} \lean{frohmanianTether_feedback} assumes a finite-dimensional real inner-product space of dimension at least $2$, and $\kappa\ge0$, $w\ge0$. \lean{feedback_fixes_coefficient} identifies the free coefficient $c$ with $\kappa w$ from the Route-A-style feedback predicate. \lean{routeA_determines_kappa} assumes $w\neq0$ and the \lean{RouteAFeedback} hypothesis, and concludes $\kappa=S$.
\item \textbf{Uniqueness (C4/C5).} \lean{uniqueness_of_minimal_metric_tether} assumes a finite-dimensional real inner-product space of dimension at least $3$, $u\neq0$, and a predicate $C3$ that fixes the coefficient among the family $\mathfrak{T}_F[c,1,u]$. Under those hypotheses, every admissible correction (symmetric, C1, C2, and $C3$) equals $\mathfrak{T}_F[\kappa,w,u]$. The result is not proved in infinite dimension, and not proved for an arbitrary $C3$ that does not fix the coefficient.
\item \textbf{Chevalley--Eilenberg closure.} $d_2\circ d_1=0$ holds for every linear functional (\lean{CE.d2_d1}). A $2$-cochain that is a coboundary is a cocycle (\lean{CE.cocycle_of_coboundary}). If the symmetric part of a bilinear term is alternating and $2$ is invertible, that symmetric part is zero (\lean{CE.symPart_not_cochain}): the symmetric tether is not a CE cochain.
\item \textbf{Jacobi in special cases.} \lean{jacobiator_eq_zero_of_central} assumes a classical bracket $P$ for which $u$ is central ($P(u,c)=0$), $P$ is homogeneous in the first slot, and $\Pi_u(P(a,b))=0$; under those hypotheses the tethered jacobiator vanishes. \lean{FieldJacobi.shearY_jacobi_holds} is the $x$-only, divergence-free shear class at $u=(\sin y,0,0)$. \lean{tethered_jacobi_identity} does not prove cancellation: it rewrites the jacobiator from two assumptions $h_{\mathrm{ident}}$ and $h_{\mathrm{vanish}}$, so the vanishing itself remains an open hypothesis of that theorem.
\item \textbf{Majorant and quartic absorption.} Given a differential inequality $y'\le C y^2-\kappa'' y^3$ on $[0,T)$ with $\kappa''>0$, $y(0)\ge0$, and the stated continuity and one-sided derivative hypotheses, $y(t)\le\max(y(0),C/\kappa'')$ on $[0,T]$ (\lean{comparison_bound}, \lean{layer2_bound}, \lean{Riccati.tethered_ceiling}). Without absorption, an explicit Riccati solution blows up (\lean{Riccati.riccati_blowup}). The differential inequality for a Navier--Stokes solution is not proved here.
\item \textbf{Maximum-principle feedback.} At a spatial maximum of $|\omega|$, the viscous term is nonpositive when $\nu\ge0$ and $\omega$ is twice differentiable in each coordinate direction (\lean{viscous_nonpos_at_max}). A continuous periodic function on the torus attains its maximum (\lean{periodic_exists_max}).
\item \textbf{Route-A uniqueness, and global regularity only under open hypotheses.} \lean{frohmanian_symplectic_tether_theorem} is not a smoothness theorem: in finite dimension at least $3$, with $S\ge0$, $w>0$, $u\neq0$, $\omega\perp u$ and $\|\omega\|=1$, it is the equivalence between admissibility for Route~A and $B=\mathfrak{T}_F[\kappa,w,u]$ with $\kappa=S$. \lean{frohmanian_symplectic_tether_theorem_global} packages that uniqueness together with degeneracy and a smoothness conclusion that assumes $\kappa''>0$, the named hypotheses \lean{NSTetherEnstrophyInequality}, \lean{BKMContinuation} and \lean{LocalContinuation}, all of which remain open. Neither theorem resolves the Clay problem.
\end{itemize}

\section{Refuted claims}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The tether damps the energy budget.} This is refuted. Let $B$ be a symmetric bilinear form on a real vector space $V$ with $B(x,x)\le0$ for all $x\in V$ (C3), and let $h\in V$ stand for $\delta H$. Then $B(h,h)<0$ if and only if $B(m,h)\neq0$ for some $m\in V$. Consequently, if $B(m,h)=0$ for all $m$ (C2), then $B(h,h)=0$, so the tether contributes nothing to $dH/dt$; and if $B(h,h)=0$, then $B(m,h)=0$ for all $m$: \lean{ObstructionAudit.energy_damping_iff_not_degenerate}, \lean{ObstructionAudit.bilin_kernel_of_nonpos_of_self_zero}.
\item \textbf{Jacobi vanishes on the reduced (divergence-free) orbit.} This is refuted whenever the coupling is nonzero. In the cross-product model, if $\kappa w\neq0$ then the tethered jacobiator of $(e_2,e_2,e_3)$ at $u=e_1$ is nonzero (\lean{JacobiWitness.tethered_jacobiator_ne_zero}). In the field model, if $\kappa w\neq0$ then the tethered jacobiator at the divergence-free field $u=(\sin y,0,0)$ fails on the divergence-free triple $(0,0,\sin x)$ (\lean{FieldJacobi.shearY_jacobi_fails_transverse}, \lean{FieldJacobi.divFree_aZ}). The dissipative part is nonpositive on real fields when $\kappa w\ge0$ (\lean{FieldMetriplectic.dissF_nonpos}): the bracket is metriplectic, not Poisson.
\item \textbf{The shear example shows the reduced orbit is necessary.} This is partly refuted. The failure for the non-divergence-free shear $u=(\sin x,0,0)$ is correct when $\kappa w\neq0$ (\lean{FieldJacobi.shearX_jacobi_fails}, \lean{FieldJacobi.not_divFree_uX}), but divergence-free fields also fail, as in the previous item.
\end{itemize}

\section{Open and not formalized}
The following have no Lean counterpart and remain open:
\begin{itemize}
\item Alain's Curve (\S2);
\item the Frohmanian Curve $J_{\mathrm{red}}=0$ (\S4), for which the compilation gives no explicit $J_{\mathrm{red}}$;
\item the symplectic, Melnikov, spectral, and holographic representations (\S5);
\item the explicit majorant ODE $y'=Cy^2\sqrt{y^3}+\tfrac{\pi}{4}C_{\mathrm{abs}}$.
\end{itemize}
The Clay-standard global regularity claim is open.

\section{Conclusion}
The C1--C3 architecture of the abstract model, the nine-term Jacobi expansion, and the listed special-case and refutation theorems are machine-checked. The kernel-model degeneracy theorem is machine-checked only under the three hypotheses recorded above. The compilation's two central dynamical claims, reduced-orbit Jacobi vanishing and energy-budget damping, are false as stated. Global regularity remains conditional on the open hypotheses recorded in Section~4. This paper makes no Millennium Prize claim.
